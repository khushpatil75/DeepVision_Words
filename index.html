Project Highlights

  • End-to-End Recognition Reads whole handwritten words as continuous sequences without requiring manual character slicing.
  • Robust Processing Designed to handle variations in human handwriting, including continuous top-lines (Shirorekha) and modifiers.
  • Real-Time Inference Processes images quickly via a lightweight, asynchronous backend architecture.

💡 Upload Guidelines

For the best extraction accuracy, ensure your handwritten Devanagari words are:

  • Written clearly on a plain, unlined background
  • Well-lit with high contrast (e.g., dark ink on white paper)
  • Cropped specifically to the individual word level
